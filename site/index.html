Cohort flex stack

Total upward and downward flexibility available across the cohort, with the price signal overlay. The gap between flex_up (teal) and flex_down (rust) shows the aggregate demand-shift envelope.

Price-response curve

Net import (kW) versus buy price signal seen ($/kWh), faceted by NEM region. The negative slope makes the demand-response curve estimable from open data for the first time.

Envelope compliance heatmap

Proportion of 5-minute intervals where the optimiser setpoint was within the DNSP envelope, by postcode prefix and hour of day. Green = fully compliant. Red = frequent violations.

Counterfactual ledger

Cumulative demand-response savings in AUD. The formula is asymmetric: net-importing intervals use the buy price ($/kWh); net-exporting intervals use the export price ($/kWh). This correctly accounts for negative feed-in tariff periods.

Buy/sell spread

Hourly mean buy price (teal) and sell/export price (gold) by NEM region, in $/kWh. Intervals where the export price went negative (negative feed-in tariff) are highlighted in red. As middle-of-day FiT collapses, this spread quantifies the asymmetry that demand-response programmes must account for.

Negative FiT detected in hour(s) for this region. During these intervals, exporting households were effectively paying to export.
