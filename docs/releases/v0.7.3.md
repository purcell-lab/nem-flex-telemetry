## New

- **Missed intervals are rebuilt from Home Assistant history (#40).**
  - **Automatic catch-up:** on the first start after this update, the integration compares the last 10 days with what this household has already published, rebuilds every missing 5-minute interval from the recorder's history, and publishes it. It runs once per version, in the background, after Home Assistant has started.
  - **On demand:** the new service `nem_flex_telemetry.rebuild_intervals` takes an optional `start`, `end` and `dry_run`, and returns a summary of what was checked, rebuilt and skipped, with reasons.
  - **Same records as live:** rebuilt intervals use the same record builder and validation as live ones, read the recorded sensor values at the same point in each interval, and never overwrite an interval that is already published.
  - **Nimbus source:** missing records are recovered from the recorded history of `sensor.nimbus_flex_telemetry`.

## Why

Before v0.7.2, intervals priced above $20/kWh were discarded. During the NSW1 price spikes on 10 and 12 October that lost real intervals. Other intervals are missing after restarts, push failures or unavailable inputs. This release recovers them while the recorder still holds the data.

## Upgrade notes

- **Update soon:** recovery only reaches back as far as the recorder keeps history, which is 10 days by default (`purge_keep_days`). Intervals from 10 October can be recovered until about 20 October.
- **Restart needed:** restart Home Assistant after updating through HACS. The catch-up starts by itself within about 15 minutes of startup; check the log for a `rebuild` summary line.
- **Not recoverable:** entities excluded from the recorder have no history to rebuild from, and nothing before the integration was set up or consent was given is published.
- **Schema unchanged:** the schema stays at v2.0. Rebuilt records are identical in shape to live ones.
