## Fix

- **Price-spike intervals are no longer dropped (#37).**
  - **The fault:** `price_signal_seen` and `price_export_seen` were limited to $20/kWh. The 2026-27 NEM market price cap is $20.70/kWh, and retail prices add GST and network charges on top. During the NSW1 spikes on 10 and 12 October, prices of about $23/kWh failed validation, and those intervals were skipped and never published.
  - **The fix:** the market price window is now -2.0 to 30.0 $/kWh, which leaves room for the annual CPI indexation of the cap. A price in $/MWh by mistake is still rejected.

## Upgrade notes

- **Schema unchanged:** the schema stays at v2.0. The wider upper bound is backward compatible.
- **Restart needed:** restart Home Assistant after updating through HACS.
- **Already-dropped intervals:** intervals skipped before this update are not recovered by this release. A rebuild-from-history service that recovers them from the Home Assistant recorder is coming in the next release. Recovery only works while the recorder still holds the data, which is 10 days by default.
